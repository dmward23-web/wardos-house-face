Ward House Privacy Policy

Ward House is a private, personal home dashboard used by one household on its own screens. It is not a public service and has no sign-ups.

Google data we use

With the household's permission, Ward House reads the household's own Google Nest camera list and live camera streams. It does this only through Google's Smart Device Management API, and only to show them on the household's own screens.

What we keep and share

Ward House stores no video. It does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.

Tokens

The sign-in tokens Google issues stay on the household's own computer. They are never published.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

Ward House's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the household owner.
