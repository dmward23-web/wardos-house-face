| Fact | Law |

## What the glass shows

1. **Home is Family sheets** (`sheet-index.html`). Header: WARDOS · HOUSE · WARD · **Family sheets**.
2. **One leave-by bar.** Next leave-by only. Date · time in the light/gold pill · place · one detail line · day badge. Charcoal band. This is the loudest line on the wall.
3. **Tiles, not a list.** Wide frosted cards, **5 across**, two full rows plus a third peek. Icon, name, one subline, tap affordance. Kid tiles keep kid color (Ainsley purple, Hayes green, Harris blue).
4. **Type from the hallway.** Leave-by time is the biggest type on the plate. Tile names readable at a walk-by. No body paragraphs. No fine print as the point.
5. **Camo + WardOS mark.** Soft white/grey glass camo. Inter Display. Frost shells from the kit.
6. **Tap opens the sheet** on the same glass (week, month, kid, chores, dinner, pack). Every sheet keeps **‹ Sheets** back to this home.

## What never goes on this glass

- Dollar amounts, balances, card owed, Autopay, Ledger FREEZE / BUY
- Therapy notes, medical detail, Harbor drafts, texts to a person
- Desk dark shell (`#070b10`), Vita meters, homework-scold chrome
- Browser UI, cursors, "check in" 15-minute blocks, 9:00 Midwest Anxiety
- A second home, a fifth board, or a phone layout stretched to 27"

Dad Seat / desk gate may exist as a tile. It stays a grown-ups boundary mock. **No dollar amounts** even there. Stars, not cash, on allowance.
